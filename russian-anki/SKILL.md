# Russian Anki

Use this as a Russian-specific layer over Anki Core. These conventions are **inferred defaults**
because no Russian deck was available in the project snapshot.

- Preserve Cyrillic exactly and include transliteration only if the learner’s established deck
  uses it; do not make transliteration replace Cyrillic.
- For nouns/adjectives, record gender and the relevant declension or case pattern.
- For verbs, distinguish aspect, infinitive, present/future behavior, past forms, and motion-verb
  prefixes when relevant.
- Include stress marks when pronunciation or homography makes them useful; keep an unmarked form
  too when that matches the intended answer.
- Use case-governing prepositions and natural examples; show the case or aspect being tested.
- Label colloquial, formal, archaic, regional, and borrowed vocabulary.
- Avoid cards whose answer is ambiguous without a case, aspect, or stress cue.
